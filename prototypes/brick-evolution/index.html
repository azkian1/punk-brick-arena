Parts and validation

The head is the original VIOLET model with 423 parts. New parts come from the other 16 models. Each keeps its size, type and original color. Placement does not depend on color. New body sections are hollow with internal supports. Smooth tiles remain in reserve; the body uses bricks and plates.

"Donor heads opened" counts the complete donor sets needed to supply the required sizes; some parts remain in reserve. These counts describe this assembly experiment, not the game's loot economy. The two panels show separate completed blueprints. Their body geometry is now used by the game's evolution system, which assembles bodies from actual collected parts.

The reserve contains every unused donor part, including smooth tiles. The balance is verified: installed body parts + reserve = all donor parts. The Reserve button shows the complete stock as a pile. Its arrangement is illustrative, without falling physics. In the game, the player's reserve is reconsidered alongside new loot between rounds; these prototype donor sets are never granted to the player.

All parts are checked for intersections and connectivity to the core under the game's rules. These are static digital assemblies: physical LEGO clutch strength, load capacity, articulated joints and animation have not been validated.
